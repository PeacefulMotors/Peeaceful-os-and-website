How We Work

Peaceful Motors  ·  Plain and simple  ·  Updated August 3, 2026

The short version: we come to you, you get a written price before we touch anything, and nothing gets added to your bill without you saying yes first.

What we do

Mobile auto, diesel, and small engine work — diagnostics, repair, maintenance, and inspections, done wherever your vehicle sits. Every visit includes a $20 service call, and you know that up front. That $20 is what replaces a tow bill.

What it costs

  • General labor: $150/hour.
  • BMW, MINI, Mercedes-Benz: $170/hour.
  • Diesel and commercial: $225/hour, two-hour minimum — same rate nights, weekends, and holidays.
  • More than 25 miles out: add $1.50/mile, quoted before we head your way.
  • Card and digital payments carry a 3% processing fee.

Diagnostics

We charge to diagnose because finding the problem takes real time and real equipment. $40 of that comes right back off the bill when you have us do the repair. Once we've started diagnosing, that charge is earned.

Deposits — based on the size of the job

  • Under $300: no deposit. You pay when it's done.
  • $300 to $999: deposit covers the parts, that's it.
  • $1,000 and up: parts plus 25% of the labor estimate.
  • Special-order parts are paid in full up front — those can't go back.

Your deposit comes off your final bill. It's not extra money. Change your mind more than 48 hours out and we haven't ordered parts yet? Full refund. If parts are already ordered, you get back whatever the supplier credits us, all of it. And if we can't finish the job for a reason that's on us, you get every dollar back.

Scheduling

Call 314-919-7456, email us, or book online. We need 48 hours to cancel or move an appointment — less than that and there's a $50 fee, because that slot was held for you and someone else could have had it.

If we show up and can't work — vehicle's locked, the spot isn't safe, wrong service was booked — the service call and any diagnostic time already done still gets billed. We drove out and set up.

Parts you bring us

You're welcome to supply your own. We'll tell you straight if we think a part is wrong for the car, and we won't install anything unsafe or counterfeit. We stand behind our labor on it for 7 days; the part itself is between you and whoever sold it. If we handle a return for you, there's a 10% handling fee. Same 10% applies to parts we ordered for you that come back or get cancelled.

Warranty

Parts we supply: 12 months or 12,000 miles, part and labor both. Parts you supply: 7 days on our labor. Something goes wrong, call us with your invoice number and we'll look at it ourselves. Full details are on our warranty page.

Your service history

We report completed repairs to CARFAX, which protects your resale value and backs up any future warranty claim. If you'd rather a particular repair stay off it, just tell us before we invoice.

A few things we need from you

  • A safe, legal place to work. If it isn't, we may have to reschedule.
  • Permission for us to drive your vehicle for testing — that's how we confirm the fix.
  • Approving an estimate by text, email, or payment counts the same as signing it.

Where we stand on liability

If something goes wrong on a job, what we owe is capped at what you paid us for that job. We can't cover lost income, rental cars, or a tow somebody else arranged. And like any shop in Missouri, we can place a lien on a vehicle if a bill goes unpaid — that's the law, not a threat. Pay your invoice and it never comes up.
