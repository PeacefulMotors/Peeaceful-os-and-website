Your Privacy

Peaceful Motors  ·  Plain and simple  ·  Updated August 3, 2026

The short version: we keep the info we need to fix your car, bill you, and stand behind the work. We never sell it, and we never hand it to advertisers. Period.

What we keep

  • How to reach you. Name, phone, email, and where the car is.
  • Your vehicle. Year, make, model, VIN, mileage, plate, and what we've done to it.
  • The job. What you told us, what we found, estimates, invoices, and our notes.
  • Photos and video. Of the car and the work, so we can both see what was done.
  • Payment. Handled by Stripe. Your card number never touches our systems.
  • Location. The address you give us, and where our tech is while heading to you.

What we do with it

We use it to schedule your job, order the right parts, do the work, send you the estimate and the bill, and honor your warranty down the road. Your service history is what makes a warranty claim easy — that's why we keep it.

Who ever sees it

  • Parts suppliers get vehicle details so the right part shows up.
  • Stripe handles payment, and the financing company if you choose financing.
  • The services that deliver our texts and emails to you.
  • Vehicle history services get completed repairs — that helps your car's resale value. Don't want a repair reported? Tell us before we invoice it.
  • Nobody else, unless a court makes us.

Texting

We only text about your car — appointments, estimates, invoices. No marketing, ever. Reply STOP any time and the texts end. Reply HELP if you need a hand.

How long we hold onto things

Job and payment records stay for seven years — that covers taxes and your warranty. Photos, texts, and emails go after about three years. If something's in dispute, we keep the file until it's settled.

It's your info

Want a copy of what we have? Want something fixed or deleted? Call 314-919-7456 or email peacefulmotors@outlook.com and we'll take care of it within 30 days.

How we protect it

Records live on encrypted, password-protected systems, and only our staff can get to them. Card numbers stay with Stripe, never with us.
