Our Warranty

Peaceful Motors  ·  Plain and simple  ·  Updated August 3, 2026

If we supplied the part, you're covered

12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever comes first — the part and the labor.

If a part we installed fails, we replace it and we don't charge you again for the work. Coverage starts the day on your invoice.

If you brought your own part

We stand behind our work on it for 7 days. The part itself came from your supplier, so if the part fails, that's between you and them — and putting in its replacement is a new job. If we think a part you brought isn't right for the car or isn't safe, we'll tell you before we touch it, and we won't install anything unsafe.

Want longer coverage?

We offer extended coverage on qualifying repairs. Just ask when we do the work — we'll put it in writing on the spot.

Diagnostics

The diagnostic fee pays for the time and equipment it takes to find the problem. And $40 of it comes right back off the bill when you have us do the repair.

What the warranty doesn't cover

We cover our parts and our work. We can't cover things outside of that, like:

  • Stuff that wears out on every car — brake pads, wiper blades, filters, belts, and fluids.
  • Accidents, floods, fire, theft, or vandalism.
  • Racing, off-roading, overloading, or towing more than the truck is rated for.
  • Skipped maintenance, or driving on after you knew something was wrong.
  • A different problem we pointed out that you chose not to fix — and anything that failure causes.
  • Work somebody else did, and damage that came from it.
  • Rust and cosmetic wear.

A few things that end coverage

  • Somebody else works on or modifies the system we repaired.
  • The car keeps getting driven with a warning light, leak, noise, or overheating tied to our repair.
  • The original bill hasn't been paid.

Something wrong? Here's what to do

  • Call us at 314-919-7456 with your invoice number. That's it — no forms.
  • If driving it would make things worse, park it and call. We come to you.
  • We'll take a look ourselves — we do our own diagnosis before we approve the fix.
  • If it's on us, we make it right at no charge, mobile service call included.
  • The warranty follows the car and the customer we did the work for.
