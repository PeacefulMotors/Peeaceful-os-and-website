Know before we go. Paid repair work begins only after a written estimate or documented scope is approved. If hidden damage changes the scope, work pauses until the additional amount is approved.

1. Agreement and services

These Terms govern mobile automotive, diesel, fleet, inspection, small-engine, diagnostic, and consulting services provided by Peaceful Motors, LLC. Requesting service, approving an estimate, signing an authorization, or making payment means you accept the version of these Terms in effect for that service.

Availability depends on vehicle condition, location, weather, worksite safety, tooling, parts, and whether the work can be completed responsibly in the field. Peaceful Motors may recommend towing, sublet work, or a properly equipped facility when mobile service is not appropriate.

2. Estimates and authorization

  • You receive the expected scope and price before paid repair work begins.
  • Your electronic signature, written approval, or clear electronic acceptance authorizes the described work.
  • You authorize reasonable vehicle operation for diagnosis, road testing, quality checks, and service delivery.
  • Additional work requires a supplement or updated approval. Approval of one repair does not authorize unrelated work.
  • An estimate is based on what can reasonably be known before disassembly. Hidden damage, broken fasteners, prior repairs, access problems, or newly discovered faults may change the price or schedule.

3. Booking, holds, cancellations, and access

  • A service request is not confirmed until Peaceful Motors accepts the date, time window, location, scope, and any required booking hold.
  • The standard booking hold is $50 and is credited in full to the related job.
  • The booking hold is non-refundable if the customer cancels or no-shows. Peaceful Motors may choose to reschedule or credit it in writing, but it is not automatically refundable.
  • A missed appointment, inaccessible vehicle, unsafe site, or materially incorrect booking information may use the booking hold to cover reserved time and dispatch cost.
  • You must provide lawful access to the vehicle and a stable, reasonably level, safe work area. Keys, wheel locks, security codes, and required permissions must be available.

4. Published prices, travel, and deposits

The current public price board is at peacefulmotors.com/prices. Current standards include $150/hr Standard, $175/hr European, $195/hr Diesel with a two-hour minimum, $275/hr Exotic, and $80/hr Small Engine labor. Diagnostic levels are $165, $295, and $495, with a $40 credit toward the related approved repair.

  • Eligible after-hours work generally carries a 40% labor uplift. Diesel after-hours labor uses the posted $250/hr rate.
  • Travel is $40 for 30 to 45 driving miles and $80 for 45 to 50 driving miles from the published service base. Longer trips are quoted individually before dispatch.
  • For jobs over $1,000, the standard job deposit is parts in full plus 25% of estimated labor. The exact dollar amount is shown before collection.
  • Special-order and non-returnable parts may require full payment before ordering.
  • Supplier restocking charges up to 10% may be deducted from a parts refund when a customer cancels or changes approved work after ordering.

5. Parts and customer-supplied parts

Peaceful Motors may decline any part that appears unsafe, counterfeit, damaged, incompatible, incomplete, or unsuitable for the repair. Parts supplied by the customer are installed as-is. They are not covered by the Peaceful Motors Confidence Warranty. Unless the written estimate states otherwise, customer-supplied-part installation carries a seven-day workmanship check on installation only, not the part or replacement labor after the part fails.

Removed parts may be returned when requested in advance and when supplier, core, warranty, hazardous-material, or legal requirements do not require another disposition.

6. Limited warranty

Qualifying repairs using qualifying new parts supplied and installed by Peaceful Motors may receive the 24-month/24,000-mile Peaceful Motors Confidence Limited Warranty, whichever limit occurs first. The invoice and Warranty Record identify whether a repair qualifies and control if they state a more specific coverage term.

Diagnostics alone, wear items, customer-supplied or used parts, maintenance, preexisting or unrelated faults, misuse, neglect, collision, environmental damage, unauthorized alteration, continued operation after a warning, and work performed by others are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

7. Inspections and consulting

An inspection reports conditions reasonably observable at the stated time, place, and selected inspection tier, usually without disassembly. It is not a guarantee against hidden or future failure, a title or history report, an emissions or government certification unless expressly stated, or a promise that the vehicle is fit for every purpose.

Consulting provides practical analysis, documentation, workflow, or estimate review within the agreed scope. It does not replace legal, tax, engineering, insurance, manufacturer, or licensed professional advice. Additional consulting terms are available in the Consulting Agreement.

8. Invoices and payment

  • Diagnostic charges are earned when diagnostic work begins.
  • Deposits and holds are applied as described on the estimate or invoice.
  • Final payment is due according to the invoice. Available payment methods and any processor-specific terms are disclosed before payment.
  • You remain responsible for amounts not paid by an insurer, warranty administrator, fleet account, lender, or other third party unless Peaceful Motors agrees otherwise in writing.
  • Peaceful Motors may use rights and remedies available under applicable Missouri law for authorized unpaid labor, services, skill, materials, or storage.

9. Site safety and customer responsibilities

You are responsible for accurate vehicle and location information, lawful access, pets and bystanders kept clear, and disclosure of known hazards, modifications, previous damage, fluid leaks, security devices, or conditions that could affect safe service. Work may stop if conditions become unsafe. Work already performed and non-returnable costs remain payable.

10. Limits, liability, and Missouri law

The separate Limits & Liability page is part of these Terms. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Peaceful Motors is not responsible for losses caused by preexisting conditions, inaccurate customer information, inaccessible or unsafe sites, unrelated failures, customer-supplied parts, third-party work, supplier delay, weather, or continued operation after a warning.

Any limitation applies only to the extent allowed by law. Nothing in these Terms waives a customer right or Peaceful Motors responsibility that cannot legally be waived or limited. Missouri law governs these Terms, without creating a promise that every dispute must be handled in a way prohibited by applicable law.

11. Privacy, communications, and updates

The Privacy Policy explains how customer, vehicle, service, media, location, and payment-related information is handled. Service texts and emails are used for appointments, estimates, authorization, invoices, job updates, and warranty matters. Reply STOP to end optional texts.

Terms may be updated for future services. The version connected to an accepted estimate or confirmed booking governs that transaction unless a later written agreement says otherwise.
