Quick customer rules

Written approval before repair

A service request or inspection does not authorize repair. Paid work begins after the written scope and amount are approved. Hidden damage means stop, document, and request a supplement.

Booking and deposits

The standard $50 booking hold is credited to the related job. Jobs over $1,000 normally require parts in full plus 25% of estimated labor. Special-order or non-returnable parts may require advance payment.

Posted rates

Standard $150/hr; European $175/hr; Diesel $195/hr with a two-hour minimum; Diesel after hours $265/hr; Exotic $275/hr; Small Engine $80/hr. Diagnostic levels are $165, $295, and $495 with a $40 related-repair credit.

Travel and after hours

Travel is $40 for 30–45 driving miles and $80 for 45–50 driving miles. Longer trips are quoted before dispatch. Most eligible after-hours labor adds 40%; diesel uses the posted $265/hr after-hours rate.

Customer-supplied parts

They may be declined for safety, quality, fit, or completeness and are not covered by the 24/24 Warranty. Unless stated otherwise, installation receives a seven-day workmanship check on installation only.

Safe mobile worksite

The vehicle must be lawfully accessible on a stable, reasonably level surface, away from traffic and avoidable hazards. Weather or unsafe conditions may require rescheduling, towing, or relocation.

Payment and records

Diagnostic charges are earned when diagnostic work begins. Final payment follows the invoice. Estimates, approvals, supplements, invoices, payment records, and warranty notes are retained as one connected service record.
