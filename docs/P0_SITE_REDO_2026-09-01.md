## Homepage structure
1. Compact header with Services, Prices, Book, Call.
2. Human hero: `Mobile auto repair that comes to you.`
3. Four compact service rows: Diagnostics, Mobile Repair, Diesel/Fleet, Inspections.
4. Three-step workflow: tell us the vehicle, approve the written work, we come to you.
5. Trust strip: written approval, photo findings, 24/24 qualifying warranty, Greater St. Louis.

7. Short owner/about section in natural local-business voice.
8. Quiet Peaceful Ministries mission line and footer.
